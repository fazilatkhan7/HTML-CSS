Why Gelato Is Not Ice Cream

Less air

Gelato is churned slowly, so it usually holds far less air than ice cream. The result is denser and more intense.

Less fat

It leans on milk more than cream, which lets fruit and nut flavours come through without being coated.

Served warmer

It is kept slightly less cold than ice cream, so it melts on the tongue and carries more flavour.

Our Story

Fior & Fiore borrows from fior di latte, Italian for flower of milk, and fiore, the word for flower. It began in 2021 as a weekend experiment, and every flavour is still made in small batches from a recipe we refine each season.
